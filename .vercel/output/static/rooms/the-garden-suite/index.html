About this room

The Garden Suite is our most generous room — a full retreat within a retreat. Floor-to-ceiling louvred shutters open onto a private terrace where bougainvillea climbs the stone wall and a ceiling fan stirs the warm air. Timber beams, hand-laid mosaic floors, and locally woven textiles give the space a grounded, unhurried quality that invites you to slow down. The bathroom is a highlight in itself: a deep soaking tub sits beneath a skylight, and the open-air shower is screened by a garden hedge rather than glass. Breakfast is served on your terrace each morning — you set the time.
